Fill the rest

One CSV turns this into a complete daily calendar back to March 9. Two ways to get it, fastest first.

  1. Manual export, two minutes. In freshbros.com wp-admin go to Analytics → Revenue, set the date range to Mar 9, 2026 – today, set interval to Day, click Download. Drop the CSV in Drive under 01_WooCommerce Growth and tell me. I append every day into this page and into Cash Calendar LIVE as a new version.
  2. Standing automation. Allow freshbros.com in this environment's network policy and add a read-only WooCommerce REST key (Consumer Key/Secret) as environment secrets. Then this page can be refreshed on a schedule from wc-analytics/reports/revenue/stats?interval=day, no human in the loop. The SiteGround captcha noted in your calendar rules may still block server-side calls; the manual export is the safe fallback.

Either way, nothing in this page is booked as cash. Order-date sales and FCB clears stay on separate lines, exactly as the calendar rules require.
