The home

Meticulously maintained second-floor coach home on one of the prettiest water lots in Treviso Bay, walking distance to the amenity center, with a full TPC golf membership.

Welcome to 9502 Napoli Lane, a meticulously maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom coach home on an exceptionally beautiful lot with water views in the Di Napoli neighborhood of Treviso Bay. The home is walking distance to the five-star amenities.

The thoughtfully designed floor plan brings in abundant natural light. A welcoming foyer leads up to two guest rooms separated from the primary suite, a large kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a great room that opens to the screened lanai over the lake. Built-in cabinets, a laundry tub, pantry, vaulted ceilings and walk-in closets are all here.

A full TPC Treviso Bay golf membership transfers with the home.
