The home

Extensively upgraded 4-bedroom plus den single-story home with a 3-car garage, gas-heated pool and spa, and a full TPC Treviso Bay golf membership. Fully furnished.

Experience the best of the Treviso Bay lifestyle in this beautifully upgraded 4-bedroom plus den, 3-bath home with a 3-car garage and a full TPC Treviso Bay golf membership. Offered fully furnished, this move-in-ready home features an impressive array of thoughtful updates and custom improvements.

The welcoming foyer with abundant natural light draws you toward the outdoors and the spacious screened pool and spa. Inside: built-in and closet cabinetry, French doors, tray and vaulted ceilings, a laundry tub, pantry, walk-in closets and window coverings throughout. The kitchen has a gas cooktop, island and eat-in space, and the pool bath keeps wet feet out of the house.

Treviso Bay's amenities include the TPC championship course, the 64,000 square foot main clubhouse, the Villa Rilassare amenity center with the La Brezza resort pool, a staffed fitness center, tennis, pickleball, bocce and a full-service spa.
