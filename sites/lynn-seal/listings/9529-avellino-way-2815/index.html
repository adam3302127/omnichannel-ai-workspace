The home

Beautifully upgraded 2-bedroom plus den veranda with a private detached garage and an expansive lake view in the Avellino neighborhood of Treviso Bay. Offered furnished and move-in ready.

Experience the best of the Treviso Bay lifestyle in this beautifully upgraded 2-bedroom plus den, 2-bath veranda condo with a private single detached garage in the desirable Avellino neighborhood. Offered elegantly furnished, this move-in-ready condo features an impressive array of thoughtful updates and custom improvements.

The open-concept floor plan has a split-bedroom layout, so owners and guests both get privacy. From the moment you enter the light and airy first-floor condo you are drawn to the lake views. The spacious kitchen offers a new tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, a pantry and a breakfast bar. The den has pocket doors for privacy. The primary bedroom has two walk-in closets, an en-suite bath with dual sinks, and a built-in storage bench the owners added as a reading spot facing the lake.

Two no-see-um screened lanais provide a place for morning coffee or afternoon cocktails. Upgrades include new upscale lighting in almost every room, custom window coverings, updated screens, and metal water-supply connections as required by the HOA. The condo comes with a social membership for year-round resort amenities and the option to play the TPC Treviso Bay course for a fee May through October.
