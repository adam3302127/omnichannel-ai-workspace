About Treviso Bay

Treviso Bay is a guard-gated, Mediterranean-inspired resort community in South Naples, about five miles from the Gulf beaches and Fifth Avenue South. It is the only community in Naples with a TPC-designated course and a bundled equity golf membership on select homes.

Lynn Seal has closed 40 transactions inside the gates, more than in any other community in her career, and plays on the Treviso Bay tennis team. She knows which lots were hand-picked for lake views, which floor plans hold value, and what a golf versus social membership does to a sale price and annual dues.

Questions buyers ask

Treviso Bay FAQ

What is the difference between a golf membership and a social membership in Treviso Bay?

Homes with a bundled TPC golf membership include full golf privileges with the purchase and carry higher annual dues. Social-membership homes include the clubhouse, pools, fitness, tennis and dining, and owners can play the TPC course for a fee during the off season, typically May through October. Some resales carry a transferable golf membership. The type attached to a home changes its price and its buyer pool, so confirm it before you list or write an offer.

What home types are in Treviso Bay?

Estate and single-family homes on Firenze, Piacere, Italia Way and Corso Bello; coach homes in Di Napoli and Veneto; verandas in Avellino and Venezia; and terrace condos in Giaveno, Acqua and Trevi.

How far is Treviso Bay from the beach?

About five miles to the Gulf beaches and Naples Pier, and about six miles to Fifth Avenue South.

What do the HOA fees cover?

Master association fees cover cable and internet, irrigation water, grounds maintenance, security, street lights, trash and reserves. Condo fees add building insurance, water and sewer. Club dues are separate and depend on membership type. Lynn can send the current fee schedule for any address.
