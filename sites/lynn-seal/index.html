About Lynn

Trained in medicine. Fluent in golf communities. Relentless about the details.

Lynn earned her Biology degree at Purdue University and spent her first career in the medical field, in sales, in private practice, and in physical therapy. Those years taught her to listen carefully, document everything, and follow through. She brings the same discipline to every contract, inspection, and closing.

Lynn and her husband David moved from Michigan to Naples after raising Lynn's three children, who now live in three different states. She knows firsthand what it takes to relocate to Southwest Florida: the logistics, the seasonal timing, the questions about flood zones, HOA fees, golf memberships, and whether to buy turnkey furnished. Her clients get answers from someone who has lived those decisions.

She is known for consistent communication, strong attention to detail, and making sure every party in a transaction has a positive experience. When she is not working, you will find her on the tennis courts or the golf course near her parents, family, and friends.

Community spotlight

Treviso Bay, explained by someone who lives there

Treviso Bay is the only community in Naples with a TPC-designated golf course and a bundled equity golf membership on select homes. It is Mediterranean-inspired, guard-gated, and about five miles from the beach and Fifth Avenue South. It is also where Lynn has closed 40 transactions, more than any other single community in her career.

That depth matters. Lynn knows which lots were hand-picked for lake views, which floor plans (Bougainvillea, Bromelia, Bellini) hold value, how the golf versus social membership question changes a buyer pool, and what the transfer and club fees actually total at closing. She plays on the Treviso Bay tennis team and sees the clubhouse, La Brezza pool, and Villa Rilassare as a member, not just an agent.

Full TPC golf membership, social membership, or transferable golf? The answer changes your price, your buyer pool, and your annual dues. Lynn will walk you through all three before you list or write an offer.
