About Lynn

Medical-field discipline. Golf-community fluency. Michigan roots.

Lynn earned her Biology degree at Purdue and spent her first career in medicine, in sales, private practice, and physical therapy. It taught her to read every line and follow through on every detail. She brings the same rigor to contracts, inspections, and closings.

She and her husband David moved from Michigan to Naples after raising Lynn's three children. She knows the relocation questions firsthand, from seasonal timing and flood zones to which golf membership actually fits your life. When she is not working, she is on the tennis court or the golf course.
